4.2 Bearing capacity of the pad footing

The footing is 2.0 m square and founded 1.5 m below ground on medium dense sand with a unit weight of 18 kN/m². With the water table below the zone of influence, the ultimate bearing capacity follows the Terzaghi expression for a square footing.

The allowable pressure is taken as the ultimate value divided by a factor of safety of 3.0, which gives 310 kN 310 kPa against an applied pressure of 185 kPa.

Settlement under the service load is estimated at 12.4 mm, within the 25 mm limit for isolated footings on sand.

How a message runs

One message, one undo.

The Assistant works through the same tools you do, on the document in your window, not on a copy. Each step shows in the chat as a row with its arguments, its result and how long it took. When the message ends, everything it changed folds into a single undo step.

  1. Reads
    get_text find_text get_outline to find the passage, render_page to look at figures and scans.
  2. Marks
    markup_text highlights, underlines or strikes the exact words and pins a comment beside them, saved as a real PDF annotation.
  3. Edits
    replace_text retypes printed words on the same baseline and size. batch groups edits and rolls them all back if one fails.
  4. Checks
    render_page again on every page it changed, so overflow and overlaps are caught before you see them.
  5. Hands back
    A short summary by page. Keep it, or press Undo once.

The agent edits in your window, not behind your back.

A real editor underneath.

The chat sits beside a small, fast PDF editor with a Photoshop-style interface. Everything the Assistant does, you can also do by hand.

The Assistant panel

Review a paper in one message.

Pick a provider, paste a key or point it at your vLLM server, and ask. Drop pictures, PDFs or text files on the chat to give it more to work with.

Type on the page

Real, selectable text in Thai and Latin, whiteout, shapes, ink, paths and pictures.

Comments that travel

Sticky notes and markups are saved as PDF annotations that Acrobat and Preview show.

Sign it

Drawn signatures, and digital signatures with your own certificates, checked on open.

Fix the pictures

Levels, exposure, hue and saturation, crop, healing and clone on the pictures inside the PDF.

Pages, in any order

Reorder in the Page Grid, rotate, delete, insert, merge another file, and drag pages between windows.

For agents

Bring your own agent.

PDFflow is also an MCP server. Connect Claude Code, Claude Desktop or any MCP client and it drives the open window with the same tools the Assistant uses.

  • 52 tools: documents, text, markup, pages, objects, pictures, signatures.
  • Arguments checked against the schema; a typo comes back with the valid names.
  • Several documents at once, one window each, addressed by id.
  • Ready prompts: review_pdf and edit_pdf.
Terminal 
# connect Claude Code (starts PDFflow when needed)
claude mcp add --scope user pdfflow -- /Applications/PDFflow.app/Contents/MacOS/pdfflow --mcp

# or over HTTP while PDFflow is open
claude mcp add --scope user --transport http pdfflow http://127.0.0.1:7878/mcp

# print both for your copy of PDFflow
pdfflow --mcp-config
